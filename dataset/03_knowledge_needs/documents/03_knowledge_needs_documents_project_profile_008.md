# Trayectorias Educativas: an applied study for acompañamiento temprano

**Project ID:** PRJ-008  
**Status:** COMPLETED  
**Period:** 2026–ongoing

## Problem
Existing information about trayectorias educativas is fragmented across academic and institutional sources, limiting timely analysis.

## Summary
This project investigates trayectorias educativas in relation to acompañamiento temprano, using analítica educativa. It focuses on institutional evidence and reproducible decision support.

## General objective
Develop and evaluate an approach based on analítica educativa to connect evidence related to trayectorias educativas and acompañamiento temprano.

## Methodological notes
Applied study combining data integration, analítica educativa, validation and traceable analysis.

## Expected results
Expected outputs include validated evidence, prioritized findings and a reproducible analytical component.

## Institutional context
acompañamiento temprano
