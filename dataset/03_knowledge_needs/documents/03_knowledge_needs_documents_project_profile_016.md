# Secuencias De Aprendizaje: an applied study for perfil del estudiante

**Project ID:** PRJ-016  
**Status:** COMPLETED  
**Period:** 2018–2019

## Problem
Existing information about secuencias de aprendizaje is fragmented across academic and institutional sources, limiting timely analysis.

## Summary
This project investigates secuencias de aprendizaje in relation to perfil del estudiante, using analítica educativa. It focuses on institutional evidence and reproducible decision support.

## General objective
Develop and evaluate an approach based on analítica educativa to connect evidence related to secuencias de aprendizaje and perfil del estudiante.

## Methodological notes
Applied study combining data integration, analítica educativa, validation and traceable analysis.

## Expected results
Expected outputs include validated evidence, prioritized findings and a reproducible analytical component.

perfil del estudiante
