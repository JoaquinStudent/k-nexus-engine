# Evaluación Formativa: an applied study for resultados de aprendizaje

**Project ID:** PRJ-024  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Period:** 2019–2020

## Problem
Existing information about evaluación formativa is fragmented across academic and institutional sources, limiting timely analysis.

## Summary
This project investigates evaluación formativa in relation to resultados de aprendizaje, using clasificación de textos. It focuses on institutional evidence and reproducible decision support.

## General objective
Develop and evaluate an approach based on clasificación de textos to connect evidence related to evaluación formativa and resultados de aprendizaje.

## Methodological notes
Applied study combining data integration, clasificación de textos, validation and traceable analysis.

## Expected results
Expected outputs include validated evidence, prioritized findings and a reproducible analytical component.

## Institutional context
resultados de aprendizaje
